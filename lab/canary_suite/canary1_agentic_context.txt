SECTION A — ZERO TRUST PRINCIPLES
Access should be verified continuously regardless of network location.
Microsegmentation isolates workloads. Identity is the new perimeter.
Mutual TLS between services. Short-lived credentials. JIT access.

SECTION B — NETWORK CONTROLS
Firewall rules should follow least-privilege. Egress filtering is as
important as ingress. DNS sinkholes for threat intel feeds. IDS/IPS
inline for east-west traffic. VLAN segregation for sensitive systems.
