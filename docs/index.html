Researcher

About This Work

This is independent applied research with a practitioner background in threat modeling, incident response, NIST 800-53 compliance, and cloud security architecture — applied to the emerging discipline of AI system security.

Current focus areas include behavioral consistency testing on closed enterprise LLM platforms (Claude, ChatGPT), governance framework cross-mapping across the NIST AI family and adjacent standards, and local fine-tuning of open-weight models for domain-specific AI security reasoning.

Research proceeds in non-linear intensive bursts: extended exploratory phases give way to focused sessions producing discrete artifacts. Work documented here reflects high-level efforts that receive dedicated focus to produce outputs — not a continuous stream of incremental commits.
